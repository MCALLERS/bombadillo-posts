Our Story

Crafting Holiday Magic Since 2018

At Blue Heron, we believe holiday decor should be more than decoration — it should be an experience. Our team of artisans, engineers, and storytellers design each animatronic figure from the ground up, combining old-world craftsmanship with modern animation technology.

Every piece is hand-painted, quality-tested, and built to create moments of wonder for years to come. From the glow of LED eyes to the realistic motion of articulated limbs, we obsess over every detail so you can focus on making memories.
